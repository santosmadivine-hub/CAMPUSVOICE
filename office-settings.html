CampusVoice
Version 1.0 · ISUFST-SEC

CampusVoice is a mobile and web-based school evaluation system for the Iloilo State University of Fisheries Science and Technology – San Enrique Campus (ISUFST-SEC). It lets students, staff and visitors evaluate campus offices and facilities, and gives each office its own dashboard, transaction codes and analytics.
